Welcome to A.M. Jain College

The Campus Feedback System helps students share their opinions about college facilities and services. The administration can review feedback and identify areas that need improvement.

How to Submit Feedback

1. Open the Student Feedback page.

2. Enter your name.

3. Select a college facility.

4. Choose a star rating.

5. Add a message if you wish. It is optional.

6. Click Submit Feedback.
